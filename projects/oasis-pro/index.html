OASIS PRO, CES Stimulation Medical Device

In 2022, my group members and I created a simulated stimulation medical device with QT. Working together we were able to implement all its features and displayed it through a graphical interface...

What is OASIS PRO?

The OASIS Pro is a cutting-edge, premium Cranio-Electro Stimulation (CES) gadget that stimulates the head with brief pulses of electricity. Through the application of modest electrical stimulus to the head, OASIS Pro encourages mental and physical healthiness. It can be used while sitting, lying down, or performing other calm, risk-free activities.

Project Technologies/Strategies:

  • 🖥️ C++ - QT Framework
  • 💻 QT.io
  • 🖳 qSql & PostgreSQL
  • 📃Agile Design
  • 📄UML Diagrams

Project Approach:

Worked in and created an agile environment with 4 team members to tackle the term project, daily scrums were conducted to reach effective incremental & iterative development of the Oasis Pro system & its functionality. User sessions were stored & managed from a connected SQL Database to further integrate robustness within the system.
